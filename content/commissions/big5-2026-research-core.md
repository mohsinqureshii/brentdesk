## The event

- **Big 5 Construct Saudi 2026**, 30 August – 2 September 2026 (four days).
- **Venue:** Riyadh Front Exhibition & Conference Center, at ROSHN Front,
  King Khalid International Airport, Riyadh 13413.
- **Patronage:** the Ministry of Industry and Mineral Resources.
- **Opened by:** Eng Fawaz bin Badr Al Shora, Deputy Minister of Industry
  and Mineral Resources for Industrial Services and Compliance.
- **Scale:** more than 1,000 exhibitors from over 50 countries; the
  organiser's stated visitor expectation is 35,000+ trade visitors.
- **Theme:** "From foundation to future, built for every scale".
- **Sector scope:** general construction, concrete, heavy equipment,
  HVACR, facilities management.
- **Co-located:** Heavy Saudi Arabia, Totally Concrete Saudi Arabia,
  HVACR Saudi Arabia, Saudi FM & Clean.

## Big 5 Impact Trail awards — three winners, all verified

| Award | Winner | For |
|---|---|---|
| Sustainability Innovation | Prime Middle East Trading Company (PRIMECO) | Solstice® low-GWP refrigerant solutions |
| Best Circular Economy Solution | Masdar Building Materials | Kronospan products |
| Operational Sustainability | Al-Futtaim Engineering Company | Integrated facilities management solution |

The Impact Trail spotlighted solutions from Masdar Building Materials,
Galaxy Technology, Star Factory, Keller and Al-Futtaim Engineering,
recognising low-impact technologies, circular-economy principles and
operational sustainability.

## Named exhibitors confirmed in official/trade material

- **Masdar Building Materials** — Kronospan carbon-negative wood-based panels
  and engineered boards. This is the Saudi building materials distributor
  founded in 1971, part of the Al-Muhaidib Group, with a branch network across
  the Kingdom. It is NOT Masdar (Abu Dhabi Future Energy Company), the UAE
  renewable energy developer that appears elsewhere in the archive. Always
  name it in full.
- **Galaxy Technology** — Albion Valves, with carbon transparency across its cast-iron range
- **Star Factory** — spray polyurethane foam roofing and waterproofing
- **Keller** — ground improvement and foundation solutions
- **Al-Futtaim Engineering** — integrated facilities management and energy management

## Conference programme — confirmed

Topic areas: codes, standards and regulations; health and safety;
sustainability; artificial intelligence; technology; project management;
concrete; modular construction; HVACR.

Sessions address navigating the Saudi Building Code, improving concrete
quality, strengthening project governance, applying AI in project delivery,
and improving coordination between HVACR design, installation and FM.

- **Christopher Seymour**, Managing Director, Middle East & Africa,

Their sessions examine collaboration between stakeholders, digital
transparency, procurement challenges and lessons from Saudi Arabia's major
developments.

## Rules for writers

1. **A session listing is not a quotation.** The programme says what a
   session would cover. It does not say what anyone said. Do not attribute
   remarks to a speaker without post-session evidence — a speaker post,
   attendee report, video or transcript.
2. **Two named speakers only.** Christopher Seymour and Shonagh Kinnaird are
   the only individuals confirmed here. Any other named person needs its own
   verification.
3. **PRIMECO's legal name** is Prime Middle East Trading Company. Use it on
   first mention.
4. **Do not inflate the scale figures.** 1,000+ exhibitors, 50+ countries.
   The 35,000 visitor figure is the organiser's expectation, not a result —
   attribute it as such unless a post-show number is verified.
5. Anything not in this file must be verified independently before use.
