# Final Candidate 100: Saudi Human Resources Ministry Digital Launches

**Proposed headline:** Saudi Human Resources Ministry Brings New Digital Services and AI-Led User Experience to LEAP 2026

**Event relation:** LEAP 2026

**Announcement date:** 2026-08-31

The Saudi Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development said its LEAP 2026 programme includes the launch of digital products and beneficiary-facing solutions, demonstrations of its existing digital services in an interactive pavilion, and strategic memoranda of understanding intended to develop digital services and improve user experience. The ministry frames the programme around artificial-intelligence adoption and government institutional excellence. This is a distinct public-sector digital-services story and does not duplicate Jisr’s private HR-and-finance platform launch or the Saudi Digital Government Authority’s Digital Experience Maturity Index.
