# TechScoop Desk Editorial Standard: LEAP and DeepFest 2026

Each draft must read like original, carefully edited newsroom work. The copy should be direct, specific and measured, with natural sentence variation and clear transitions. Avoid promotional language, generic AI-era clichés, repeated event boilerplate, keyword stuffing, padded conclusions, invented colour, or assertions that are not supported by the supplied research.

## Article shape

Write an 850–1,250-word article body, excluding the reference list. Open with a concrete Riyadh news lead that states what happened, who announced it and why it matters. Follow with the material details and numbers, then place the development in company, institutional, Saudi or MENA context. Explain practical implications for customers, competitors, investors, public services or infrastructure as appropriate. Include a measured limitations or “what happens next” paragraph when the announcement leaves material questions unanswered.

Use two to four informative subheadings. Paragraphs should usually contain two to five sentences. Do not use bullet lists, tables, Q&A formatting, or repetitive “In conclusion” endings. Do not write advertorial copy. Attribute analysis and projections to the source or identify them explicitly as TechScoop analysis.

## Factual discipline

Treat the supplied `verified_key_facts` and `verified_angle_summary` as the authoritative claim boundary. Do not add a number, launch date, investment value, product capability, customer count, quotation or executive title unless it is in those facts or directly supported by one of the supplied URLs. Never invent a quotation. When a source release makes a claim, attribute it to the company or institution instead of presenting it as an independently verified outcome.

Link factual attributions naturally in the body using Markdown links. Finish with a `## References` section using numbered Markdown reference definitions. Include the primary source first and supporting sources after it. Do not cite TechScoop’s own unpublished blueprint or generated files.

## SEO and discoverability

Use the assigned focus keyword naturally in the headline or first 120 words and one additional location where it fits. Integrate secondary keywords semantically; do not force exact phrases. Preserve a useful search-intent match, distinct headline, concise excerpt, SEO title of no more than 65 characters and description of no more than 165 characters. Mention LEAP 2026 or DeepFest 2026 accurately, and maintain the Saudi Arabia/Riyadh context where relevant.

## Entity integrity

Use the assigned company, institution, people and event names exactly enough for reliable backend entity linking. Do not add a named person merely to decorate the story. If the research contains no verified named person, write the article without one. Event, company, person, category, topic, tag, geography and keyword associations remain structured metadata; the prose should mention only entities that materially belong in the story.

The author is **TechScoop Desk**. Every item is an unpublished draft dated 31 August 2026, with the assigned Asia/Riyadh time between 12:00 PM and 11:59 PM. Do not describe the article as published, live, approved or exclusive. Do not include any instruction to publish it.
