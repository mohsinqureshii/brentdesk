## 3. ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES

### 3.1 Initial Roles

| Founder | Title | Primary Responsibilities |
|---------|-------|-------------------------|
| [Founder 1] | CEO | Overall strategy, fundraising, business development |
| [Founder 2] | CTO | Product development, engineering, technology |
| [Founder 3] | COO | Operations, finance, HR |

### 3.2 Time Commitment
Each Founder agrees to devote their full business time and attention to the Company. No Founder shall engage in any other business activity without prior written consent of the other Founders.

### 3.3 Role Changes
Changes to Founder roles require unanimous consent of all Founders.

---

## 4. DECISION MAKING

### 4.1 Major Decisions
The following decisions require unanimous consent of all Founders:
- Raising capital or taking on debt
- Hiring or firing executive employees
- Entering into contracts over $[X]
- Changing the Company's business direction
- Selling or licensing intellectual property
- Acquiring another company
- Selling the Company

### 4.2 Day-to-Day Decisions
Each Founder has authority to make decisions within their area of responsibility, subject to the budget approved by all Founders.

### 4.3 Deadlock Resolution
If Founders cannot reach agreement on a major decision after good faith discussion:
1. Mediation with a mutually agreed mediator
2. If unresolved, binding arbitration

### 5.3 Work for Hire
All work created by Founders in the course of their duties shall be "work for hire" and owned by the Company.

---

## 6. CONFIDENTIALITY

### 6.1 Confidential Information
Each Founder agrees to maintain the confidentiality of all Company information and not to disclose such information to any third party without prior written consent.

### 6.2 Duration
Confidentiality obligations survive termination of this Agreement indefinitely.

---

## 7. NON-COMPETITION AND NON-SOLICITATION

### 7.1 Non-Competition
During employment and for [1] year after departure, Founders agree not to engage in any business that competes with the Company.

### 7.2 Non-Solicitation
During employment and for [2] years after departure, Founders agree not to solicit Company employees or customers.

---

## 8. DEPARTURE OF A FOUNDER

### 8.1 Voluntary Departure
If a Founder voluntarily leaves:
- Unvested shares are forfeited
- Vested shares are subject to Company's right of first refusal
- Departing Founder must provide [30] days notice

### 8.2 Termination for Cause
If a Founder is terminated for cause:
- All unvested shares are forfeited
- Company may repurchase vested shares at fair market value
- "Cause" includes fraud, gross negligence, or material breach

### 8.3 Death or Disability
If a Founder dies or becomes disabled:
- All shares immediately vest
- Estate/Founder retains all shares

---

## 9. DISPUTE RESOLUTION

### 9.1 Mediation
Any dispute shall first be submitted to mediation.

### 9.2 Arbitration
If mediation fails, disputes shall be resolved by binding arbitration under [AAA/JAMS] rules in [CITY, STATE].

### 9.3 Governing Law
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.

---

## 10. GENERAL PROVISIONS

### 10.1 Entire Agreement
This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Founders regarding the subject matter hereof.

### 10.2 Amendments
This Agreement may only be amended by written consent of all Founders.

### 10.3 Severability
If any provision is found unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in effect.
