## CHARTER

### Dividends
The holders of the Series A Preferred Stock shall be entitled to receive dividends at the rate of [8]% per annum, payable when and if declared by the Board of Directors.

### Liquidation Preference
In the event of any liquidation, dissolution or winding up of the Company, the holders of Series A Preferred Stock shall be entitled to receive in preference to the holders of Common Stock an amount equal to [1x] the Original Purchase Price plus any declared but unpaid dividends (the "Liquidation Preference").

### Conversion
Each share of Series A Preferred Stock shall be convertible, at the option of the holder, at any time, into shares of Common Stock at the then-applicable Conversion Rate.

### Anti-Dilution
The Conversion Price of the Series A Preferred Stock shall be subject to adjustment on a broad-based weighted average basis.

### Voting Rights
Each share of Series A Preferred Stock shall have a number of votes equal to the number of shares of Common Stock then issuable upon conversion.

## INVESTOR RIGHTS

### Board of Directors
The Board shall consist of [5] members:
- [2] designated by holders of Series A Preferred Stock
- [2] designated by holders of Common Stock
- [1] independent member mutually agreed upon

### Information Rights
The Company shall deliver to each Major Investor:
- Annual audited financial statements within 120 days of fiscal year end
- Quarterly unaudited financial statements within 45 days of quarter end
- Annual budget within 30 days of fiscal year start

### Registration Rights
**Demand Rights:** Holders of at least [50]% of the Registrable Securities may demand registration.
**Piggyback Rights:** Investors shall be entitled to "piggyback" registration rights.
**S-3 Rights:** Investors shall be entitled to unlimited S-3 registrations.

### Right of First Refusal
Major Investors shall have a pro rata right to participate in subsequent equity financings.

---

## OTHER TERMS

### Employee Pool
Prior to closing, the Company shall reserve shares of Common Stock for issuance to employees, directors and consultants such that the total option pool equals [15]% of the fully-diluted capitalization.

### Vesting
All stock and stock equivalents held by founders and employees shall be subject to vesting over [4] years with a [1] year cliff.

### Drag-Along
Holders of Series A Preferred Stock and Common Stock shall be required to vote in favor of any acquisition approved by the Board and holders of a majority of the Series A Preferred Stock.

### No-Shop
The Company agrees not to solicit, initiate, encourage or accept any other proposals for financing for a period of [45] days.
