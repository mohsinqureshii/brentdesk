## Slide 2: Problem

### The Problem We're Solving

**Key Questions to Answer:**
- What pain point exists in the market?
- Who experiences this problem?
- How significant is this problem?

**Template:**
> "[Target customer] struggles with [specific problem], which costs them [time/money/opportunity]."

**Tips:**
- Use data to quantify the problem
- Tell a story that resonates
- Make it personal and relatable

---

## Slide 3: Solution

### Our Solution

**Key Questions to Answer:**
- How do you solve the problem?
- What makes your solution unique?
- Why is now the right time?

**Template:**
> "[Product name] enables [target customer] to [key benefit] by [how it works]."

**Tips:**
- Keep it simple and clear
- Focus on the "what" not the "how"
- Show, don't tell (use visuals)

---

## Slide 4: Product Demo

### How It Works

**Include:**
- Screenshots or product mockups
- Key features highlighted
- User flow visualization

**Tips:**
- Show the product in action
- Highlight 3-4 key features
- Keep it visual, minimal text

## Tips for a Great Pitch Deck

1. **Keep it concise** - 12-15 slides max
2. **Tell a story** - Problem → Solution → Why Now
3. **Use visuals** - Less text, more images
4. **Know your numbers** - Be ready for questions
5. **Practice** - Rehearse until it's natural
6. **Customize** - Tailor to each investor
