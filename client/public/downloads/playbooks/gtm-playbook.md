## 1. GTM Fundamentals

### What is Go-to-Market?

A Go-to-Market (GTM) strategy is your plan for launching a product or entering a new market. It answers:

- **Who** are your target customers?
- **What** problem do you solve?
- **How** will you reach them?
- **Why** should they choose you?

### The GTM Framework

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                    GTM STRATEGY                         │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  MARKET          │  PRODUCT         │  DISTRIBUTION    │
│  - Segmentation  │  - Positioning   │  - Channels      │
│  - Targeting     │  - Messaging     │  - Sales Model   │
│  - Sizing        │  - Pricing       │  - Partnerships  │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### GTM Motions

| Motion | Description | Best For |
|--------|-------------|----------|
| **Product-Led** | Product drives acquisition | Self-serve, freemium |
| **Sales-Led** | Sales team drives deals | Enterprise, complex |
| **Marketing-Led** | Marketing generates demand | Mid-market, SMB |
| **Community-Led** | Community drives adoption | Developer tools, B2D |

## 2. Market Analysis

### Market Segmentation

**Segmentation Criteria:**

| Criteria | B2B Examples | B2C Examples |
|----------|--------------|--------------|
| **Firmographic** | Industry, size, revenue | Demographics |
| **Geographic** | Region, country, city | Location |
| **Behavioral** | Usage patterns, buying behavior | Purchase history |
| **Psychographic** | Values, priorities | Lifestyle, interests |

### Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

**ICP Template:**

```
IDEAL CUSTOMER PROFILE

Company Characteristics:
- Industry: [e.g., SaaS, Fintech]
- Size: [e.g., 50-500 employees]
- Revenue: [e.g., $5M-$50M ARR]
- Geography: [e.g., North America, MENA]
- Technology: [e.g., Uses Salesforce, AWS]

Buyer Persona:
- Title: [e.g., VP of Engineering]
- Reports to: [e.g., CTO]
- Goals: [e.g., Ship faster, reduce bugs]
- Challenges: [e.g., Legacy systems, talent]
- Watering holes: [e.g., Hacker News, Twitter]

Buying Process:
- Decision makers: [Who signs?]
- Influencers: [Who recommends?]
- Budget: [Where does it come from?]
- Timeline: [How long to close?]
```

### TAM/SAM/SOM Analysis

**Definitions:**
- **TAM** (Total Addressable Market): Everyone who could buy
- **SAM** (Serviceable Addressable Market): Those you can reach
- **SOM** (Serviceable Obtainable Market): Realistic near-term target

**Calculation Methods:**

1. **Top-Down:** Industry reports × your segment %
2. **Bottom-Up:** # of customers × average deal size
3. **Value Theory:** Value delivered × willingness to pay

## 3. Positioning & Messaging

### Positioning Statement

**Template:**
> For [target customer] who [need/pain point], [product name] is a [category] that [key benefit]. Unlike [competitors], we [key differentiator].

**Example:**
> For engineering teams who struggle with deployment complexity, Vercel is a frontend cloud platform that enables instant deployments. Unlike traditional hosting, we provide zero-config deployments with automatic scaling.

### Messaging Framework

| Level | Purpose | Example |
|-------|---------|---------|
| **Tagline** | Memorable hook | "Ship faster" |
| **Value Prop** | Core benefit | "Deploy in seconds, scale automatically" |
| **Proof Points** | Evidence | "Used by 100K+ developers" |
| **Features** | Capabilities | "Automatic HTTPS, global CDN" |

## 4. Pricing Strategy

### Pricing Models

| Model | Description | Best For |
|-------|-------------|----------|
| **Freemium** | Free tier + paid upgrades | PLG, high volume |
| **Usage-Based** | Pay per use | API, infrastructure |
| **Per-Seat** | Price per user | Collaboration tools |
| **Tiered** | Good/Better/Best | Most SaaS |
| **Flat Rate** | Single price | Simple products |

### Pricing Psychology

**Key Principles:**
1. **Anchoring:** Show higher price first
2. **Decoy Effect:** Add option to make target look better
3. **Price Endings:** $99 vs $100
4. **Bundling:** Combine features for perceived value

### Value-Based Pricing

**Steps:**
1. Identify the value you create
2. Quantify that value in dollars
3. Price at 10-20% of value delivered
4. Test and iterate

**Example:**
- Your tool saves 10 hours/week
- Engineer costs $100/hour
- Value = $1,000/week = $4,000/month
- Price = $400-$800/month

## 5. Channel Strategy

### Channel Options

**Direct Channels:**
- Outbound sales
- Inbound marketing
- Product-led growth
- Events and conferences

**Indirect Channels:**
- Channel partners
- Resellers
- Marketplaces
- Affiliates

### Channel Selection Framework

| Factor | Weight | Channel A | Channel B |
|--------|--------|-----------|-----------|
| CAC | 25% | 8/10 | 6/10 |
| Scalability | 25% | 7/10 | 9/10 |
| Control | 20% | 9/10 | 5/10 |
| Speed | 15% | 6/10 | 8/10 |
| Fit | 15% | 8/10 | 7/10 |
| **Total** | 100% | **7.5** | **7.1** |

### Sales Model Selection

| ACV | Model | Team |
|-----|-------|------|
| <$1K | Self-serve | Product, Growth |
| $1K-$10K | Inside sales | SDRs, AEs |
| $10K-$100K | Field sales | AEs, SEs |
| >$100K | Enterprise | Named accounts |

## 6. Launch Execution

### Pre-Launch Checklist

**4 Weeks Before:**
- [ ] Finalize positioning and messaging
- [ ] Create launch assets (website, demo, docs)
- [ ] Brief internal teams
- [ ] Prepare press/analyst outreach
- [ ] Set up tracking and analytics

**2 Weeks Before:**
- [ ] Test all systems
- [ ] Train sales and support
- [ ] Seed content with influencers
- [ ] Prepare launch day communications
- [ ] Plan contingencies

**Launch Day:**
- [ ] Publish website updates
- [ ] Send announcements
- [ ] Post on social media
- [ ] Monitor for issues
- [ ] Engage with community

### Launch Tiers

| Tier | Effort | Activities |
|------|--------|------------|
| **Tier 1** | Major | Press, event, campaign |
| **Tier 2** | Medium | Blog, email, social |
| **Tier 3** | Light | Changelog, in-app |

### Launch Amplification

**Channels:**
1. Product Hunt
2. Hacker News
3. Twitter/X
4. LinkedIn
5. Reddit (relevant subreddits)
6. Industry newsletters
7. Podcasts
8. Webinars

## 7. Measuring Success

### GTM Metrics

**Awareness:**
- Website traffic
- Social mentions
- Press coverage
- Share of voice

**Acquisition:**
- Leads generated
- Sign-ups
- Demo requests
- Trial starts

**Activation:**
- Activation rate
- Time to value
- Feature adoption
- NPS

**Revenue:**
- Pipeline generated
- Win rate
- Deal size
- Sales cycle length

### Iteration Framework

**Weekly:**
- Review leading indicators
- Adjust tactics
- A/B test messaging

**Monthly:**
- Analyze channel performance
- Review win/loss data
- Update forecasts

**Quarterly:**
- Strategic review
- ICP refinement
- Pricing analysis
