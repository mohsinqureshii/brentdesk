## 1. When to Raise

### Signs You're Ready

**You should raise when:**
- You have a clear vision and can articulate it
- You have some evidence of product-market fit
- You know exactly how you'll use the funds
- You can demonstrate momentum

**You should NOT raise when:**
- You're desperate for cash
- You have no traction or clear path to traction
- You can bootstrap to the next milestone
- Market conditions are unfavorable

### The Ideal Timing

> "The best time to raise money is when you don't need it."

**Fundraising Timeline:**
- Start preparing 2-3 months before you need money
- Active fundraising takes 2-4 months
- Always have 6+ months of runway when you start

## 2. How Much to Raise

### The Goldilocks Principle

**Too Little:**
- Constant fundraising mode
- Can't execute on plans
- Weak negotiating position

**Too Much:**
- Excessive dilution
- Pressure to spend
- Higher expectations

**Just Right:**
- 18-24 months of runway
- Clear milestones to Series A
- Reasonable dilution (15-25%)

### Calculating Your Raise

```
Target Raise = Monthly Burn × 18 months + Growth Buffer

Example:
- Current team: 3 people
- Planned hires: 5 people
- Monthly burn: $50,000 → $150,000
- Average burn: $100,000/month
- Target: $100K × 18 = $1.8M
- With buffer: $2M
```

### Seed Round Benchmarks (2024-2026)

| Stage | Amount | Dilution | Valuation |
|-------|--------|----------|-----------|
| Pre-Seed | $250K-$1M | 10-15% | $2-5M |
| Seed | $1M-$4M | 15-25% | $5-15M |
| Seed+ | $3M-$6M | 15-20% | $15-30M |

## 3. Financing Options

### SAFE (Simple Agreement for Future Equity)

**Pros:**
- Simple, fast to close
- No interest or maturity date
- Founder-friendly terms
- Standard documents

**Cons:**
- Dilution unclear until conversion
- Some investors prefer priced rounds
- Can accumulate multiple SAFEs

**Key Terms:**
- **Valuation Cap:** Maximum valuation for conversion
- **Discount:** Percentage discount to next round price
- **MFN:** Most Favored Nation clause

### Convertible Notes

**Pros:**
- Familiar to traditional investors
- Interest accrues (tax benefit)
- Clear maturity date

**Cons:**
- More complex than SAFEs
- Interest creates additional dilution
- Maturity date pressure

**Key Terms:**
- **Interest Rate:** Typically 4-8%
- **Maturity Date:** Usually 18-24 months
- **Conversion Discount:** 15-25%
- **Valuation Cap:** Maximum conversion price

### Priced Round (Equity)

**Pros:**
- Clear valuation and ownership
- Establishes governance
- Preferred by some institutional investors

**Cons:**
- More expensive (legal fees)
- Takes longer to close
- More complex negotiations

## 4. Finding Investors

### Types of Seed Investors

| Type | Check Size | Value Add | Speed |
|------|------------|-----------|-------|
| Angels | $10K-$100K | Varies | Fast |
| Angel Groups | $50K-$500K | Network | Medium |
| Micro VCs | $100K-$500K | High | Medium |
| Seed VCs | $500K-$2M | High | Slower |
| Accelerators | $100K-$500K | Very High | Fast |

### Building Your Investor List

**Tier 1: Dream Investors (10-15)**
- Perfect fit for your space
- Strong track record
- Can lead the round

**Tier 2: Great Fits (20-30)**
- Good fit for stage and sector
- Active at seed stage
- Valuable network

**Tier 3: Potential Fits (30-50)**
- Invest at seed stage
- Some relevant experience
- Worth a conversation

### Getting Introductions

**Best Sources:**
1. Portfolio founders of target investors
2. Other founders they've met with
3. Mutual connections on LinkedIn
4. Accelerator networks
5. Angel investors who know them

**Cold Outreach (Last Resort):**
- Keep it short (3-4 sentences)
- Lead with traction
- Make the ask clear
- Follow up once

## 5. The Pitch

### The 30-Second Pitch

> "[Company] helps [target customer] [solve problem] by [solution]. We've [traction] and are raising [amount] to [milestone]."

**Example:**
> "Stripe helps developers accept payments online with just a few lines of code. We've processed $1M in transactions and are raising $2M to expand to 10 new countries."

### Pitch Deck Structure

**Slide 1: Title**
- Company name, logo, tagline
- Your name and contact

**Slide 2: Problem**
- What pain point exists?
- Who experiences it?
- How big is the problem?

**Slide 3: Solution**
- How do you solve it?
- What's unique about your approach?

**Slide 4: Product**
- Screenshots or demo
- Key features

**Slide 5: Market**
- TAM/SAM/SOM
- Market trends

**Slide 6: Business Model**
- How you make money
- Unit economics

**Slide 7: Traction**
- Key metrics
- Growth rate
- Notable customers

**Slide 8: Team**
- Founders' backgrounds
- Why you'll win

**Slide 9: Competition**
- Competitive landscape
- Your differentiation

**Slide 10: Ask**
- How much you're raising
- Use of funds
- Key milestones

### Common Questions to Prepare

1. Why are you the right team?
2. What's your unfair advantage?
3. How will you acquire customers?
4. What are your unit economics?
5. Why raise now?
6. What are the biggest risks?
7. How did you arrive at your valuation?
8. What happens if this doesn't work?

## 6. Negotiating Terms

### Key Terms to Negotiate

**Valuation Cap (SAFEs)**
- Higher cap = less dilution
- Don't optimize excessively
- Focus on the right partners

**Pro-Rata Rights**
- Right to maintain ownership %
- Standard for lead investors
- Can limit for smaller checks

**Board Seats**
- Typically 0 at seed
- If required, keep founder control
- Observer seats as alternative

**Information Rights**
- Quarterly updates standard
- Annual financials
- Cap table access

### What NOT to Negotiate

- Standard SAFE terms
- Minor legal points
- Things that don't matter

> "Optimize for speed and good partners, not for the last dollar of valuation."

### Red Flags in Term Sheets

- Participating preferred
- Full ratchet anti-dilution
- Excessive board control
- Unusual vesting requirements
- Redemption rights

## 7. Closing the Round

### The Closing Process

**Week 1-2: Verbal Commits**
- Get verbal commitments
- Confirm amounts
- Discuss timing

**Week 3-4: Documentation**
- Send SAFE/documents
- Collect signatures
- Answer questions

**Week 5-6: Wire Transfers**
- Send wire instructions
- Confirm receipt
- Update cap table

### Post-Close Checklist

- [ ] Update cap table
- [ ] File 83(b) elections (if applicable)
- [ ] Send investor updates
- [ ] Add investors to communication channels
- [ ] Update company records
- [ ] Celebrate (briefly!)

### Investor Updates

**Monthly Update Template:**

```
Subject: [Company] - [Month] Update

HIGHLIGHTS
- [Key win #1]
- [Key win #2]
- [Key win #3]

METRICS
- Revenue: $X (+Y% MoM)
- Users: X (+Y% MoM)
- Burn: $X/month
- Runway: X months

CHALLENGES
- [Challenge #1]
- [Challenge #2]

ASKS
- [Specific ask #1]
- [Specific ask #2]

Thanks,
[Name]
```

---

## Key Takeaways

1. **Raise when you have leverage**, not when you're desperate
2. **Target 18-24 months** of runway
3. **Use SAFEs** for simplicity and speed
4. **Build relationships** before you need money
5. **Practice your pitch** until it's natural
6. **Focus on the right partners**, not just the highest valuation
7. **Close quickly** once you have momentum
